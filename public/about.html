About CSKit

Your free Counter-Strike toolkit.

CSKit brings configuration tools, public player lookup, practice planning and cosmetic price tracking into one workspace. Essential tools are free, and guest use does not require an account.

Independent by design.

CSKit is a community-built project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve, Steam, Leetify or Skinport. Counter-Strike and related game artwork belong to their respective owners.

Sources and limitations.

Market listings come from Skinport. Available player statistics come from Leetify. Studio exports use a documented command registry, and its first-person preview is a 2D approximation. Practice observations and experiment results are entered by the player.
