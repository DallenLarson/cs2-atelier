Your data in CSKit

Last updated October 10, 2026.

Guest use.

Your guest wishlist, Studio profiles, practice history, experiments, map favorites and layout are stored in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes them. Export backups before switching browsers or clearing data.

Optional Google account.

Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Signed-in Studio builds, wishlist and workspace records are stored privately in Firestore under your account. Guest originals stay separate; importing is explicit. Your display name, email and profile photo are used for the account interface.

Third-party requests.

Player lookup sends the player identifier you enter to Leetify. Results are shown for the current page and are not persisted by CSKit. Market data is fetched from the public CSKit feed, sourced from Skinport. Google/Firebase, hosting and linked providers may process normal connection information according to their own policies.

Local account copies.

A signed-in workspace copy and failed-save drafts can remain in this browser to help recover work. On shared devices, export what you need and clear CSKit site storage after signing out. Signing out alone does not erase browser storage.

Control your records.

Use Workspace data to export practice and experiments, import copies, or clear that workspace. Studio has profile export and deletion. Wishlist entries can be removed in Market. The account page exports Studio and wishlist data. Self-service deletion of the Google-linked CSKit account is not yet available; account deletion support remains on the roadmap.

Public sharing and analytics.

Public profiles and squads are not enabled. This release adds no analytics trackers, advertising cookies or automatic notifications. Opening external links is your choice.
